### Q&A Preparation
**Q: How does the 6-level reasoning work?**
A: Our AI evaluates: (1) Symptom severity, (2) Vital signs correlation, (3) Urgency assessment, (4) Facility matching, (5) Care pathway planning, (6) Risk stratification. All in under 3 seconds.

**Q: What if the AI makes a mistake?**
A: The AI escalates uncertain cases (orange indicator) for human review. Only 81% are auto-approved - the clear, straightforward cases. Complex cases always get human oversight.

**Q: Can this scale to 900 million users?**
A: Yes! Serverless architecture (Lambda) auto-scales. Cost per patient remains constant at $0.0006. We've architected for horizontal scaling from day one.

**Q: What about rural areas with poor internet?**
A: The app is optimized for low bandwidth. Progressive Web App (PWA) works offline. Critical features cached locally. Syncs when connection available.
