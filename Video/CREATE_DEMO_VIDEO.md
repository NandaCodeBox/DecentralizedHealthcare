# 🎬 Quick Start: Create Demo Video

**3 Simple Steps to Create Professional Demo Video**

---

## Step 1: Generate Voiceover (2 minutes)

```powershell
cd Video
.\generate-story-voiceover.ps1
```

**Output**: `story-driven-voiceover.mp3` (professional Indian female voice)

---

## Step 2: Record Screen (3 minutes)

```powershell
python story-driven-recorder.py
```

Press Enter when ready, then sit back and watch!

**Output**: `Arogya_AI_Story_Driven_Recording.webm`

---

## Step 3: Combine Video + Audio (5 minutes)

```powershell
python combine-story-video.py
```

**Output**: `Arogya_AI_Final_Demo.mp4` (ready to upload!)

---

## Total Time: 10 minutes

Then upload to YouTube and submit! 🎉
