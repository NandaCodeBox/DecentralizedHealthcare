Meet Arogya dot A I - India's intelligent healthcare platform that breaks down barriers to quality care.

Watch how easy it is to get help, right from your mobile phone, in your own language.

First, we sign in securely. The platform supports multiple languages - English, Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu.

Let's switch to Hindi to show how accessible this is for everyone.

Now, let's describe our symptoms. We have a fever that's been going on for three days. The interface is simple and intuitive.

We select the severity - moderate - and the duration. Then submit for A I analysis.

Within seconds, our A I powered by Amazon Bedrock analyzes the symptoms and provides a triage assessment. It shows an 87 percent confidence score and recommends nearby facilities.

Each facility is ranked by A I with a match percentage, showing distance, wait times, and availability. The A I explains why each facility is recommended.

We can book an appointment directly, or view detailed information about the facility.

Now let's try the A I powered provider search. We can describe what we need in natural language - like "I have chest pain and shortness of breath."

The A I understands the urgency and recommends appropriate specialists, sorted by relevance. Each provider shows their qualifications, availability, and A I match score.

For healthcare supervisors, there's a dedicated dashboard to review and validate A I assessments, ensuring human oversight for critical decisions.
