## 🚨 TROUBLESHOOTING

### Video is Still Too Long:
- Speed up video slightly (1.1x or 1.2x)
- Cut more aggressively
- Remove pauses and waiting

### Audio Doesn't Sync:
- Adjust audio start time in editor
- Trim silence from audio beginning
- Use markers to align key moments

### File Size Too Large:
- Compress video in Kapwing
- Use H.264 codec
- Reduce bitrate slightly
- 1080p at 5-10 Mbps is good

### Export Takes Too Long:
- Use lower quality preview first
- Export final at high quality
- Close other applications
- Be patient (can take 5-10 minutes)
