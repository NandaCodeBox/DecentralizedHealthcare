# HACKATHON DEMO - STORY-DRIVEN VOICEOVER SCRIPT
# Duration: 3 minutes (180 seconds)
# Focus: Problem Statement → Solution → Impact

## SEGMENT 1: THE PROBLEM (0-30s)

Meet Rajesh. He's experiencing severe chest pain at 2 AM. In rural India, he faces a critical challenge. The nearest hospital is 50 kilometers away. He doesn't know if this is an emergency or if he can wait until morning.

This is the reality for 900 million Indians in underserved areas. Long wait times. Limited access to specialists. No guidance on urgency. Lives are at risk because of delayed care.

## SEGMENT 2: THE SOLUTION - PATIENT JOURNEY (30-75s)

Now, watch how Arogya dot AI changes everything. Rajesh opens the app on his mobile phone. He selects Hindi, his native language.

He clicks on his symptoms. Chest pain. Shortness of breath. Fever. The interface is simple. No medical jargon. Just tap and describe.

He rates the severity as severe and indicates it started 2 hours ago. Within seconds, our AI powered by Amazon Bedrock analyzes his symptoms.

The AI confidence score is 94 percent. It identifies this as a high-priority case requiring immediate attention. The system recommends three nearby facilities, ranked by AI matching scores. City General Hospital is 95 percent match, just 2 kilometers away, with a 2 hour wait time.

Rajesh books an appointment instantly. Date, time, contact number. Done. His life-threatening condition is now being addressed within minutes, not hours.

## SEGMENT 3: SUPERVISOR & AGENTIC AI (75-135s)

But here's where it gets revolutionary. Behind the scenes, our Agentic AI system is working autonomously. Let me show you the supervisor dashboard.

See this purple toggle? Agentic AI is ON. This means three autonomous agents are validating every case in real-time. No human bottleneck. No delays.

Look at these statistics. Out of 47 cases today, the AI has automatically approved 38. That's an 81 percent automation rate. Only 9 cases need human review.

Let's click on Rajesh's case. See this AI analysis box? This is six-level multi-reasoning. The AI evaluated symptom severity, vital signs correlation, urgency assessment, facility matching, care pathway planning, and risk stratification. All in under 3 seconds.

The decision? Green indicator. Auto-approved. Rajesh gets immediate care without waiting for a supervisor to manually review his case.

Now look at this case. Orange indicator. The AI escalated it for human review because it detected conflicting symptoms. This is intelligent automation. The AI knows when to act and when to ask for help.

This isn't just a demo. This is deployed on AWS. Three Lambda agents running 24/7. Powered by Amazon Bedrock. The cost? Just 6 dollars and 22 cents per month for 10,000 patients. That's 0.06 cents per patient.
